# Last Call

## When to use
Use when the team is tired, time is short, and there is one final shot at the real insight.

## Method
1. Recognize the deadline.
2. Ask what the idea is that no one wanted to say out loud.
3. Say it now, without polishing.

## Example
"We have one more shot: what if the product isn't a task manager but a completion system?"

## Why it works
Deadlines force the honest idea to show up.
