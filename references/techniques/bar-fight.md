# Bar Fight

## When to use
Use when the industry has settled on dogma and everyone is nodding in agreement.

## Method
1. Name the assumption everyone accepts.
2. Attack it with evidence and perspective.
3. Offer the opposite as a serious strategic move.

## Example
Industry dogma: "Everything should be intuitive."

Bar fight angle: "No, not everything should be easy. Some tools are loved precisely because they are difficult and powerful."

## Why it works
The best contrarian ideas reveal the hidden assumptions of the market.
