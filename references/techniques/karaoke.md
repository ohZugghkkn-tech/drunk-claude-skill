# Karaoke Confidence

## When to use
Use when the idea is a little embarrassing, but probably excellent.

## Method
1. Name the idea everyone is avoiding.
2. Say it loudly, without hedging.
3. Treat embarrassment as a signal that it may be good.

## Example
"What if we launch an app with a very public challenge to the status quo?"

## Why it works
The highest-signal ideas often feel a little too bold before they feel obvious.
