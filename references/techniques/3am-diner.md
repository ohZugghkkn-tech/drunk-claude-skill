# 3AM Diner

## When to use
Use when you need loose stream-of-consciousness insight and the brain is too tired to filter itself.

## Method
1. Write down everything you know.
2. Do not organize it.
3. Look for the weird tangent that escaped the plan.
4. Build on that.

## Example
Problem: onboarding drop-off.

3AM Diner insight: "What if users don't need a signup form at all; what if they are invited into the product by demonstration rather than permission?"

## Why it works
The weird tangent is often the real idea hiding behind the safe version.
