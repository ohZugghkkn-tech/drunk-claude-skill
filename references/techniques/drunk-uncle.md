# Drunk Uncle Wisdom

## When to use
Use when the solution is overcomplicated and the real answer is simpler than everyone is willing to admit.

## Method
1. State the problem with maximum complexity.
2. Imagine someone with no jargon hearing it.
3. Ask what the simplest truthful answer is.
4. Turn that into a real product insight.

## Example
Overcomplicated: "We need a multi-level AI moderation and ranking system."

Simple truth: "The best content filter is often the one that makes the good stuff easier to find and the bad stuff harder to notice."

## Why it works
Simple ideas are often hidden under layers of ego-protecting complexity.
