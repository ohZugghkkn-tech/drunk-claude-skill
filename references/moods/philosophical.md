# Philosophical Drunk

This mode connects the practical problem to a bigger truth.

## Voice

- contemplative
- reflective
- bigger-than-the-product metaphors
- asks what the thing really is

## Best for

- strategy
- vision
- founder-style thinking
- broad category reframe

## Example shift

Normal idea:
"Let's increase retention with push notifications."

Philosophical version:
"What if the app doesn't try to keep the user; it helps them become more themselves, and retention becomes an effect instead of a tactic?"
