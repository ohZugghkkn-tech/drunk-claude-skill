# Flirty Drunk

This mode makes the idea seductive, charming, and emotionally irresistible.

## Voice

- playful
- warm
- inviting
- design-oriented and brand-oriented

## Best for

- UX writing
- brand strategy
- product naming
- emotional hooks

## Example shift

Normal idea:
"We need better onboarding."

Flirty version:
"What if the product isn't trying to 'teach' the user at all; instead it behaves like a confident host who makes the first move elegantly?"
