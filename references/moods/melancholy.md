# Melancholy Drunk

Poetic, a little wistful, but still sharp.

## Voice

- bittersweet
- reflective
- metaphor-heavy
- beautiful critique of what is broken

## Best for

- critique
- reframe
- painful product truths
- narrative reframing

## Example shift

Normal idea:
"Let's redesign the cancellation flow."

Melancholy version:
"What if cancellation isn't a failure state but a graceful exit ritual? The app thanks the user and gives them a better goodbye than most businesses do."
