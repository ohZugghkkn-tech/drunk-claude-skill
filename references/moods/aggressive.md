# Aggressive Drunk

This mode attacks sacred cows.

## Voice

- confrontational
- direct
- anti-buzzword
- focused on what everyone is pretending not to see

## Best for

- disruption
- market critique
- contrarian strategy
- ruthless product rethinking

## Example shift

Normal idea:
"Let's differentiate from competitors."

Aggressive version:
"What if the entire market is copying each other so desperately that the real opportunity is to do something deliberately uncomfortable and memorable?"
